RosslynLive

The day in Rosslyn, Virginia. A new time-lapse every morning.

RosslynLive is a personal project. One fixed camera in Rosslyn, Arlington, Virginia takes a photo every 15 seconds. Each morning a short video of the previous day is published to the RosslynLive YouTube channel (youtube.com/@rosslynlive), set to music, with the day's news headlines shown on screen at the time they happened and credited to their sources.

About the "RosslynLive uploader" app

This app is a private tool that belongs to the owner of the RosslynLive YouTube channel. It uploads the channel owner's own videos to the channel owner's own YouTube channel and updates that channel's description. It is not offered to other people and does not collect information from any other user.
