RosslynLive uploader: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

What this app is

"RosslynLive uploader" is a private automation tool operated by the owner of the RosslynLive YouTube channel (youtube.com/@rosslynlive). It is used only by that owner, with the owner's own Google account (rosslynlive19@gmail.com).

What it accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app asks for permission to manage the owner's own YouTube channel. It uses that permission only to upload the channel's daily video and to update the channel's own description. It does not read, collect or store the personal data of any other person.

What data is stored

The app stores only the sign-in token Google gives it, in the owner's private n8n workflow account, so that the daily upload can run automatically. No Google user data is stored anywhere else, and none is sold, shared, transferred or used for advertising.

Use of Google user data

RosslynLive uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Video content

The videos show a street scene in Rosslyn, Virginia, taken by a fixed camera, together with news headlines from public news feeds that are credited to their sources.

Your choices

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
